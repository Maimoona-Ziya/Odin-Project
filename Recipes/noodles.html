VEG HAKKA NOODLES

Description

Veg Hakka Noodles is an immensely popular Indo-Chinese fusion dish featuring al dente boiled noodles stir-fried over high heat in a wok with julienned vegetables and savory sauces. Characterized by its smoky "wok hei" flavor, the dish perfectly balances the soft texture of wheat noodles with the crunch of fresh veggies.

Ingredients

  • Plain Hakka Noodles; 200-300g
  • Garlic: 6 cloves, finely minced
  • Ginger: 1 tsp, finely minced
  • Green Chilli: 1–2, slit or chopped
  • Onion: 1 medium, thinly sliced
  • Carrot: 1 medium, julienned (thin strips)
  • Capsicum (Bell Pepper): 1/2 cup, julienned
  • Cabbage: 1/2 cup, shredded
  • Spring Onion Whites & Greens: 1/4 cup, chopped
  • Soy Sauce: 2 tsp
  • Green or Red Chilli Sauce: 1.5 tsp
  • Tomato Ketchup: 1 tbsp (optional)
  • Vinegar: 1.5 tsp
  • Black or White Pepper Powder: 1/4 tsp
  • Water: 1.5–2 Litres
  • Oil: 3 tbsp (preferably neutral or sesame oil)

Steps

  1. Boil the Noodles: Bring 2 litres of water to a roaring boil with 1 tsp salt and 1 tbsp oil. Add the noodles and cook according to package instructions until al dente (roughly 5–7 minutes).
  2. Shock and Oil: Immediately strain the noodles and rinse under cold water to stop the cooking process. Drain thoroughly, toss with 1 tsp of oil to keep the strands separate, and set aside.
  3. Sauté the Aromatics: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large wok or wide frying pan over high flame. Add the minced garlic, ginger, and green chillies. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Flash Fry the Veggies: Turn the heat up to its maximum setting. Toss in the sliced onions, carrots, cabbage, and capsicum. Flash-fry for 1 to 2 minutes. The vegetables must remain vibrantly colored and crunchy—do not overcook them.
  5. Season and Sauce: Add the soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, vinegar, pepper powder, and salt. Toss quickly for 30 seconds to coat the vegetables.
  6. Combine: Tip the cooked noodles into the wok. Using two spatulas or tongs, gently toss the noodles on a high flame for 1–2 minutes until everything is evenly combined and heated through.
  7. Garnish & Serve: Mix in the spring onion greens. Turn off the heat and serve immediately while hot, either on its own or alongside Veg Manchurian.
